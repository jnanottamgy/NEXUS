## What we do

- **Explainer reels:** short videos breaking down one finance idea at a time, scripted with the relevant wing.
- **Event promotion:** announcements, countdowns and reminders for every event.
- **Event coverage:** photos, videos and highlight reels from the day.
- **Recaps:** short posts that link back to the full recap in [`updates/`](../updates/README.md).

## How content gets made

1. **Topic:** a wing or the Current Affairs & PR department suggests it.
2. **Script:** Sumitha drafts it, and the wing checks it for accuracy.
3. **Shoot:** Ishaan films it.
4. **Edit:** Lekhika cuts it.
5. **Review:** a core team member checks it against the [content rules](../CONTRIBUTING.md#content-rules) before it's posted.

Every post about investing, trading or crypto should carry a short *"educational only, not financial advice"* line.
