## What we do

- **Venues:** book halls, labs and seminar rooms well in advance.
- **Permissions:** get approvals from the faculty coordinator and college administration.
- **Equipment:** mics, projectors, extension boards, stationery, and making sure everything is returned.
- **Budgets:** estimate costs, track spending, and keep bills for reimbursement.
- **Hospitality:** refreshments, and welcoming guest speakers properly.
- **Inventory:** keep track of club property (banners, standees, merch) between events.
