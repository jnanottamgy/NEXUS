## What we do

- **Maintain this repository:** keep the roster, updates and events current, and review changes from the rest of the core team.
- **Registrations and forms:** event sign-ups, feedback forms, attendance.
- **Event tech:** projectors and screens, live quizzes, and the platform for the paper trading league.
- **Finance tools:** small open-source projects members can actually use, like SIP and EMI calculators, budget trackers and market dashboards.
- **Digital hygiene:** shared drives, access to club accounts, and handing them over cleanly each year.

## Good to know

You don't need to be from CSE or ISE to contribute. Most updates to this repo are plain Markdown and can be made from the GitHub website. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
