## Team

**All positions open.** If you follow business news every morning, or you're the person who can talk to anyone, this department needs you. [See how to apply →](../JOIN.md)

## What we do

**Current affairs**

- **Weekly news digest:** the five finance and economy stories members should know about, posted in [`updates/`](../updates/README.md).
- **Big-event alerts:** flag the Budget, RBI policy and major market moves to the relevant wing in time for them to prepare a session.

**Public relations**

- **Speakers:** reach out to professionals, alumni and faculty for talks and workshops.
- **Partners:** collaborate with other MSRIT clubs and finance clubs at other colleges.
- **Sponsors:** pitch for event sponsorship (no paid promotion of investment products, tips or courses; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#content-rules)).
- **Communication:** official emails, letters and press notes on behalf of the club.

## Works closely with

[Event Management](event-management.md) on speakers, the [Finance Literacy & Economic Inscription Wing](../wings/finance-literacy-and-economic-inscription.md) on news explainers, and [Social Media](social-media.md) on announcements.
