## Team

**All positions open.** If you design posters for fun, love clean infographics or know your way around Figma, Canva or Illustrator, we want you. [See how to apply →](../JOIN.md)

## What we do

- **Event creatives:** posters, banners, standees and certificates.
- **Social media creatives:** carousels and infographics that make finance easy to read.
- **Brand identity:** the NEXUS logo, colours, fonts and templates, kept consistent everywhere.
- **Presentation templates** for wings to use in their sessions.
- **Merch:** club T-shirts, stickers and the like.

## Works closely with

[Social Media](social-media.md) for publishing, [Event Management](event-management.md) for timelines, and every wing for content.
