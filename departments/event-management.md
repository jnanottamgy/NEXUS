## What we do

- **Club calendar:** plan the semester's events around exams, holidays and the [finance calendar](../events/README.md#finance-calendar).
- **Event ownership:** for each approved event, own the plan, timeline and run-of-show, and coordinate the other departments.
- **On the day:** manage registrations at the door, keep things on time, and handle anything that goes wrong.
- **After the event:** collect feedback, make sure a recap goes into [`updates/`](../updates/README.md), and add the event to [past events](../events/README.md#past-events).

## How to pitch an event

Anyone can propose an event using the [Event proposal form](https://github.com/jnanottamgy/NEXUS/issues/new/choose). Leadership reviews it, and once approved this team picks it up. The whole flow is on the [Departments page](README.md#how-the-departments-work-together-on-an-event).
