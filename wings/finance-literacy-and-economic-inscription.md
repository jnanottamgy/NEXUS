> Make economics make sense, and write it down. This wing is the club's explainer desk and its written voice.

## Team

**All positions open.** This wing is waiting for its first team. If you like reading the news and explaining it to friends, this is your wing. [See how to apply →](../JOIN.md)

## What we cover

- **Macroeconomics in plain language:** inflation, interest rates, GDP, the fiscal deficit, exchange rates.
- **Policy decoded:** the Union Budget, the Economic Survey, RBI monetary policy, GST changes.
- **Reading the news:** what a headline actually means for students, households and markets.
- **Writing:** explainers, newsletters and the club's long-form posts (the "inscription" in our name).
- **A shared vocabulary:** a growing club glossary of finance and economics terms.

## What we run

- **Budget Decoded**: a session on Budget day (1 February) or the day after, breaking down what changed and why.
- **RBI policy recap**: a short explainer after each Monetary Policy Committee decision.
- **The NEXUS explainer**: a regular written piece on one economic idea or news story, published in [`updates/`](../updates/README.md).
- **Econ quizzes** with the Miscellaneous Wing.
