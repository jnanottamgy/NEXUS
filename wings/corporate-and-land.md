## What we cover

**Corporate**

- **Financial statements:** reading a balance sheet, P&L and cash flow statement, and the ratios that matter.
- **Valuation:** DCF, relative valuation, and why two analysts can value the same company very differently.
- **Capital markets:** IPOs and how to read a draft red herring prospectus (DRHP), bonds, private equity and venture capital.
- **Corporate actions:** mergers & acquisitions, buybacks, dividends, demergers.
- **Company law basics:** company structures, governance, and the role of the board and auditors.

**Land & real estate**

- **Real estate as an asset:** rental yield, appreciation, and costs people forget (stamp duty, registration, maintenance).
- **Buying property:** home loans, title checks, and the role of RERA in protecting buyers.
- **Listed alternatives:** REITs and InvITs, and how they compare with owning physical property.

## What we run

- **Annual report teardowns**: picking a listed company and reading its annual report together.
- **IPO watch**: breaking down an upcoming IPO from its offer document.
- **Case study competitions**: teams analyse a real deal or business problem and present a recommendation.
- **Rent vs. buy**: a hands-on session with spreadsheets and real numbers.
