## What we cover

- **Careers in finance:** CA, CFA, CS, CMA, FRM, investment banking, consulting, equity research and fintech, and how engineers get into them.
- **Behavioural finance:** why smart people make bad money decisions, from loss aversion to herd behaviour.
- **Fintech:** UPI and India's digital payments stack, neobanks, lending apps, and where tech meets finance.
- **Sustainable finance:** ESG investing, green bonds and climate risk.
- **Global finance:** how events abroad (Fed rate decisions, oil prices, currency moves) reach India.

## What we run

- **Career talks** with professionals and alumni working in finance.
- **Finance quizzes** and fun formats that bring in members from every wing.
- **Screenings and discussions** of finance films and series.
- **Cross-wing projects** that need people from more than one wing.
