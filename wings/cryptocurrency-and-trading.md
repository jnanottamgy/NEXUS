## What we cover

- **Blockchain fundamentals:** how Bitcoin and Ethereum work, wallets, keys, stablecoins, DeFi.
- **Digital assets in India:** the regulatory and tax treatment of virtual digital assets (VDAs), and why it matters before you buy.
- **How markets work:** exchanges, order types, liquidity, market makers, settlement.
- **Analysis:** technical analysis (charts, trends, indicators) alongside fundamental analysis.
- **Derivatives basics:** futures and options, what leverage really does to gains *and* losses.
- **Risk management:** position sizing, stop-losses, drawdowns, and trading psychology.

## A word on risk

SEBI's own studies of the equity futures & options segment have found that roughly **9 in 10 individual traders lose money**. This wing teaches how trading works so that members can make informed decisions, and often the informed decision is not to trade with real money yet. We do **not** share tips, signals or "calls", and we don't promote tokens, exchanges or paid courses.

## What we run

- **Paper trading league**: a semester-long competition with virtual money, ranked on risk-adjusted returns rather than just raw profit.
- **Chart-reading workshops**: hands-on sessions on reading price action and indicators.
- **Crypto 101**: from "what is a blockchain?" to self-custody and common scams.
- **Trade post-mortems**: walking through famous market events (crashes, squeezes, collapses) and what went wrong.
