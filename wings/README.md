# Wings

[← NEXUS home](../README.md)

Each wing owns a subject area. Wings decide what members learn about, run sessions and workshops on it, and write explainers and posts for the rest of the club.

| Wing | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [Personal Finance & Investment](personal-finance-and-investment.md) | Budgeting, saving, mutual funds, equities, insurance, goal planning |
| [Finance Literacy & Economic Inscription](finance-literacy-and-economic-inscription.md) | Economics explainers, Budget & RBI breakdowns, the club's writing |
| [Cryptocurrency & Trading](cryptocurrency-and-trading.md) | Blockchain, digital assets, market analysis, risk management |
| [Corporate & Land](corporate-and-land.md) | Corporate finance, valuation, IPOs, M&A, real estate |
| [Miscellaneous](miscellaneous.md) | Careers, behavioural finance, fintech, quizzes, cross-wing projects |

The current team for each wing is listed on its page and on the [home page](../README.md#wings). Open positions are in [JOIN.md](../JOIN.md).
