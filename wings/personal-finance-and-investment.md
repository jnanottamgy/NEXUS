## What we cover

- **Money basics:** budgeting, emergency funds, the power of compounding, good vs. bad debt, credit scores.
- **Investing:** mutual funds and SIPs, index funds, direct equity, debt instruments, gold, asset allocation.
- **Protection:** term and health insurance, and why insurance and investment shouldn't be mixed.
- **Tax basics for students and first-jobbers:** reading a salary slip, the old vs. new tax regime, filing your first return.
- **Goal planning:** turning goals (a laptop, higher studies, a first home) into a plan with numbers.
- **Staying safe:** spotting Ponzi schemes, "guaranteed returns" pitches, fake trading apps and UPI frauds.

## What we run

- **Money 101** for first-years: one session covering everything you wish someone had told you at 18.
- **Your first salary**: CTC vs. in-hand pay, PF, tax, and what to do with your first paycheque.
- **Virtual portfolio challenge**: teams build and defend a long-term portfolio. Graded on reasoning, not luck.
- **Fund teardowns**: picking apart a mutual fund factsheet: expense ratio, holdings, risk, benchmark.
- **Scam-awareness drives** with the Social Media department.
