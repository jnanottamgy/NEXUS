# Resources

[← NEXUS home](../README.md)

A curated starting kit, organised by wing. Official sources come first, because when the RBI, SEBI or a company's own filing says something, that beats any summary of it.

> Everything here is for learning. Inclusion is not an endorsement of any product, platform or person, and nothing here is investment advice.

## Official sources (India)

| Source | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [Reserve Bank of India](https://www.rbi.org.in/) | Monetary policy, interest rates, banking rules, financial education |
| [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/) and [SEBI investor education](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/) | Market regulation, investor education, checking whether an adviser is registered |
| [Union Budget](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/) | Budget documents, speeches and the Economic Survey |
| [Income Tax Department](https://www.incometax.gov.in/) | Filing returns, tax calculators, current due dates |
| [Ministry of Corporate Affairs](https://www.mca.gov.in/) | Company filings and corporate law |
| [AMFI](https://www.amfiindia.com/) | Mutual fund data and investor education |
| [NSE](https://www.nseindia.com/) and [BSE](https://www.bseindia.com/) | Market data, company announcements, IPO documents |
| [MoSPI](https://www.mospi.gov.in/) | Official GDP, inflation (CPI) and other economic data |
| [Press Information Bureau](https://pib.gov.in/) | Official government announcements |
| [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) | Reporting online financial fraud (helpline **1930**) |

## Personal Finance & Investment

**Learn**

- [SEBI investor education](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/): the regulator's own beginner material.
- [AMFI](https://www.amfiindia.com/): how mutual funds work, and how to check a fund's data.
- [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/): free modules, including personal finance.

**Read**

- *Let's Talk Money* by Monika Halan: personal finance written for Indians.
- *The Psychology of Money* by Morgan Housel
- *The Little Book of Common Sense Investing* by John C. Bogle
- *The Intelligent Investor* by Benjamin Graham
- *Coffee Can Investing* by Saurabh Mukherjea, Rakshit Ranjan and Pranab Uniyal

## Finance Literacy & Economics

**Learn**

- [Union Budget](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/): read the Budget speech and the Economic Survey yourself.
- [Reserve Bank of India](https://www.rbi.org.in/): MPC statements and the RBI's financial education material.
- [Khan Academy: Economics](https://www.khanacademy.org/economics-finance-domain): free micro and macro courses.

**Read**

- *Economics: The User's Guide* by Ha-Joon Chang
- *Basic Economics* by Thomas Sowell
- *Poor Economics* by Abhijit V. Banerjee and Esther Duflo
- *Freakonomics* by Steven D. Levitt and Stephen J. Dubner

**Follow:** The Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard, The Hindu BusinessLine, Reuters.

## Cryptocurrency & Trading

**Learn**

- [Bitcoin whitepaper](https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf): the original nine-page paper.
- [ethereum.org: Learn](https://ethereum.org/en/learn/): how Ethereum, wallets and smart contracts work.
- [Zerodha Varsity](https://zerodha.com/varsity/): modules on markets, technical analysis, futures and options.
- [Income Tax Department](https://www.incometax.gov.in/): how virtual digital assets are taxed in India.

**Read**

- *Mastering Bitcoin* by Andreas M. Antonopoulos
- *Trading in the Zone* by Mark Douglas
- *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator* by Edwin Lefèvre
- *A Random Walk Down Wall Street* by Burton G. Malkiel: the case that most active trading doesn't beat the market.

## Corporate & Land

**Learn**

- [Aswath Damodaran's pages](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/): free valuation courses, spreadsheets and datasets.
- [NSE](https://www.nseindia.com/) and [BSE](https://www.bseindia.com/): annual reports and IPO offer documents (DRHPs) of listed companies.
- [Ministry of Corporate Affairs](https://www.mca.gov.in/): company law and filings.
- [Karnataka RERA](https://rera.karnataka.gov.in/): check whether a real estate project is registered.

**Read**

- *The Interpretation of Financial Statements* by Benjamin Graham and Spencer B. Meredith
- *Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies* by McKinsey & Company
- *Barbarians at the Gate* by Bryan Burrough and John Helyar
