## Finance calendar

These are the moments in the year that finance people watch. We plan sessions around them.

| When | What happens | Natural fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Late January | **Economic Survey** tabled in Parliament, the day before the Budget | Finance Literacy & Economic Inscription |
| 1 February | **Union Budget** | Finance Literacy & Economic Inscription |
| Six times a year (roughly every two months) | **RBI Monetary Policy Committee** decision on interest rates | Finance Literacy & Economic Inscription |
| Jan–Feb, Apr–May, Jul–Aug, Oct–Nov | **Quarterly results season** for listed companies | Corporate & Land |
| 15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Dec, 15 Mar | **Advance tax** instalment due dates | Personal Finance & Investment |
| 31 March | **Financial year ends**, the last day for the year's tax-saving investments if you're on the old tax regime | Personal Finance & Investment |
| July | **Income tax return filing season** for individuals (check the current due date on [incometax.gov.in](https://www.incometax.gov.in/)) | Personal Finance & Investment |
| Diwali | **Muhurat trading**, a special one-hour session on the stock exchanges | Cryptocurrency & Trading |

Exact dates shift from year to year. Always confirm them against the official source before announcing a session.
