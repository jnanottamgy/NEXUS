[← NEXUS home](README.md)

## What we look for

- **Curiosity** about money, markets or the economy. Knowing it all already isn't required.
- **Reliability:** turning up and following through matters more than experience.
- **Something you can bring:** writing, design, speaking, organising, code, or just energy.

## How to apply

1. **Talk to us.** Reach out to the President (Jnanottam), the Vice President (Abhishek), or anyone on the [core team](README.md#leadership).
2. **Or apply here.** Fill in the [Volunteer for an open role](https://github.com/jnanottamgy/NEXUS/issues/new/choose) form.

> **Heads up:** this repository is public, so anything you put in that form can be seen by anyone. Share your name, year, branch and why you're interested. **Don't** post your phone number, email address or USN. We'll find you on campus.

Positions are filled on a rolling basis. Once a position is filled, it's removed from this page and the team member is added to the [home page](README.md).
